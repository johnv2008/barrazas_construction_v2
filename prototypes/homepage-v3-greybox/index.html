07 · Schedule

07 — What actually happens to you

You'll know what's happening in your house, and when.

Six stages, described from where you're standing rather than from ours.

01

Initial conversation

A call or a message. We ask what's not working and roughly what you're hoping to spend, so nobody's time gets wasted.

02

Property visit

We walk the space with you and look at the conditions we'd be working into — access, age, what's behind the walls.

03

Scope & estimate

A written scope of what's included and what isn't. You'll know the number before you commit to anything.

04

Planning & permits

Selections confirmed and materials ordered. Where permitting applies, we coordinate it.

05

Construction

The longest stage and the one people worry about. You'll know who's coming, which rooms stay usable, and what we found when we opened things up.

06

Final walkthrough

We go through the finished work together and write down anything still outstanding before we call it done.
